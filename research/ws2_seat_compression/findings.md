# WS2 — Seat compression (bear B2): findings

Evidence against the long first. Employment = BLS OEWS, US wage-and-salary, May reference; revenue = Adobe global. Indices, not seat counts.

**1. US creative employment is shrinking; 2025 is the worst non-COVID year. WEAKENS. Confidence: high.**
Creative5 (graphic designers, art directors, SFX/animators, film/video editors, photographers) fell **−4.9%** May-2024→May-2025 to 348,240, led by graphic designers **−7.7%** (214,260→197,830, a 2019–25 low). Creative5 is −5% vs 2019; marketing occupations **+37%**. BLS's Sep-2026 OOH now projects graphic designers **−2% for 2025–35**, explicitly citing AI design tools (2024–34 vintage: +2.1%).

**2. Adobe revenue has decoupled from US creative headcount. SUPPORTS. Confidence: high (data), medium (causality).**
Digital Media revenue **+129%** FY19–FY25 vs creative5 **−5%**: revenue per US creative worker index **241** (CAGR **15.8%**); Creative-only revenue per worker CAGR **14.4%** FY19–24. In FY25 employment fell 4.9% yet DM revenue grew 11.3% (**+16.9%** per worker).

**3. −3%/yr headcount is survivable on history; −7%/yr is not. NEUTRAL. Confidence: medium.**
Per-worker growth needed for ~10% ARR: **10.0%** (flat), **13.4%** (−3%/yr), **18.3%** (−7%/yr). Achieved 14.4–15.8% CAGRs clear the −3% bar; 18.3% was reached only in COVID-distorted 2020. Projected creative5, 2028: BLS-based 348–351k; postings-based 356k; trend 340k; stress (repeat −4.9%/yr) 300k.

**4. Creative postings are depressed but no longer falling. NEUTRAL. Confidence: medium.**
Sep-2026 Indeed: Media & Comm **71**, Marketing **76**, Arts & Ent **84** vs total **103** (Feb-2020 = 100); creative avg **+4.9% YoY** after a 2025 trough; software dev +15% YoY.

**5. Agency cuts are company-specific, not sector-wide. NEUTRAL/WEAKENS. Confidence: medium.**
Big-4 headcount **−5.2%** from the 2023 peak; WPP **−14.6%** from 2022; Omnicom+IPG −8,200 in 2025; Publicis **+37%** since 2019.

Net: B2 is visible in 2025 labor data (1) but not yet in revenue (2); the long survives −3%/yr seat erosion, not −7%/yr (3).
