# WS3 findings: task-level AI exposure vs. Adobe monetization (2026-10-02)

Setup: 102 O*NET 27.2 tasks, 6 occupations (4 creative, 2 marketing), Eloundou et al. (2023) labels (human and GPT-4), classified into workflow stages (100% hand-checked; keyword rule agreed 85%). "Highly exposed" = E1 or E2 (gamma). Weights: equal within occupation; occupations by OEWS May 2021 employment.

1. **The most-exposed creative stages are seat-monetized. WEAKENS; confidence medium.** For the 4 creative occupations, ideation/concepting (100% of task weight highly exposed, human-rated), asset generation (98%) and editing/retouching/compositing (88%) rank highest, and all are served mainly by per-seat Creative Cloud/Express plans with bundled generative credits. 72% of highly exposed creative task weight sits in seat-monetized stages vs 1% in usage-monetized stages (GPT-4 labels: 62% vs 10%; equal-occupation weights: 71% vs 2%).

2. **The usage-monetized stage is thin in O*NET. WEAKENS; confidence low.** Versioning/resizing/localization (Firefly Services, GenStudio) is represented by 4 legacy prepress tasks (10% of creative weight); human raters marked 9% highly exposed (GPT-4: 100%). O*NET 27.2 omits modern resize/localize work, so this understates the usage opportunity.

3. **Creative exposure is "LLM + image tools" (E2), not direct LLM (E1). NEUTRAL; confidence high.** Human alpha: Graphic Designers 0.00 (15th pctile), Art Directors 0.07, Animators 0.05, Video Editors 0.14; gamma 0.79-0.90 (83rd-93rd pctile). Image-generation AIOE puts all four at the 98th-100th percentile. Exposure is a tooling story Adobe can own or lose.

4. **Marketing occupations are equally exposed but map to enterprise platform. SUPPORTS; confidence medium.** Marketing Managers and Market Research Analysts: human gamma 0.94/0.92 (96th/95th pctile), AIOE 89th/97th pctile. Their analytics tasks (59% of six-occupation weight, 93% highly exposed) map to Experience Platform/Analytics, not seats.

Net: the task evidence confirms exposure is concentrated in creative-seat stages, but does not show usage monetization covering those stages today.
