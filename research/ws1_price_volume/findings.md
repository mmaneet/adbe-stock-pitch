# WS1 findings – is price masking seat losses?

C&MP and BP&C subscription growth, FY25 Q1–FY26 Q3, decomposed into FX, Semrush, price (list uplift × share repriced ×
renewal phase-in) and an implied volume/mix residual. Chart: `charts/price_vs_volume_stacked.png`.

1. **Price is 25–50% of C&MP growth.** Q3 FY26 C&MP +13.0% reported; less ~1pt FX and
   ~2.9pt Semrush (est. $120M) = ~9.1% organic cc. The June-2025 CC Pro repricing (+16.7% individual, +11.1% teams, NA only) contributes 1.4 / 2.8 / 4.2pt at 10/20/30% of revenue repriced, leaving implied volume/mix of **7.6 / 6.1 /
   4.7%**. Confidence medium. **NEUTRAL-to-SUPPORTS.**
2. **Implied volume/mix is positive mid-single-digit in every quarter and scenario.** Base: 9.3% (FY25 Q1) → 7.8%
   (FY25 Q4) → 8.0% → 6.4% → 6.1% (FY26 Q3); never below 4.7% even at 30%. Zero volume would need >60% of C&MP revenue
   on the full list uplift. Confidence medium. **SUPPORTS.**
3. **The residual is falling ~3pt over six quarters** (9.3% → 6.1%) while reported growth rose 10% → 13%: FY26's
   headline acceleration is price + Semrush + FX; underlying volume/mix is decelerating. Confidence medium-low (±0.7pt from FX
   rounding and the Semrush estimate). **WEAKENS** the AI re-acceleration narrative.
4. **BP&C growth of 15–16% is almost entirely volume/mix**: no verified Acrobat/Express list change Jul-2024 to Mar-2026, so price adds ~0pt in FY26 (≤0.8pt with unverified Acrobat Standard changes); implied
   volume/mix ~14–15%. Confidence medium-high. **SUPPORTS.**
5. **The price lever is being re-used**: VIP/teams increases from 1-Jun-2026 and Acrobat Standard business from
   1-Apr-2026 add ~0.3–0.5pt now and more in FY27 as the June-2025 wave laps, so FY27 reported growth will again overstate
   volume. Confidence medium. **NEUTRAL.**

Caveat: the residual is non-price revenue (seats × up-sell × realised discounts), not a seat count; Adobe last disclosed
subscribers in FY2018. UNAVAILABLE: Semrush Q1-26 and actual Q3 FY26 contribution; FY25 Creative/Document Cloud
split; realised prices; renewal timing.
