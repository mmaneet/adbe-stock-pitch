# WS5 findings: creative-tools growth vs. Adobe share (2026-10-02)

**Question:** is the pie growing so Figma/Canva gain without Adobe shrinking, or is share leaving Adobe?
**Answer:** both. Adobe adds more creative revenue per year than Figma and Canva combined, yet cedes ~2 pts of share a year: it grows ~13% while the pool grows ~16%.

| Entity | Latest YoY | Prior | Added $/yr |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adobe C&MP (Q3 FY26) | +13.0% | +11% | $2.14B |
| Adobe BP&C (Q3 FY26) | +15.6% | +15% | $1.03B |
| Figma revenue (Q2'26) | +48.3% | +40% | $0.48B |
| Canva revenue (Q2'26) | +25.2% | +43% ARR (est.) | $0.74B |
| Midjourney CY25 (est.) | +67% | +50% | $0.20B |

1. **Share drifts away from Adobe, slowly.** Pool = Adobe C&MP + Figma + Canva run-rates ($23.8B): Adobe holds 78% of stock but 64% of the latest annualized increment; share fell 2.4 pts YoY (80.7% to 78.3%). Confidence medium (Canva revenue/ARR mix; Figma partial overlap). **WEAKENS** (mildly).
2. **Adobe is not shrinking; it re-accelerated.** C&MP 11% (Q4 FY25) to 13%; total +12.9%; ARR +11.2%; FY26 C&MP guide $18.24-18.27B. Confidence high. **SUPPORTS.**
3. **Figma is the clear share-taker.** Three quarters of acceleration to +48% ($370M), NDR 136-139%, >$100k customers +46% to 1,635, FY26 guide raised twice to $1.463-1.467B. Confidence high. **WEAKENS.**
4. **Canva is slowing hard.** ARR $4.0B end-2025 (+43% est.; 265M MAU, 31M paid) but Q2-26 revenue +25% and 2026 growth guide cut from 30% to ~20% on AI serving costs. Confidence medium (private). **SUPPORTS.**
5. **TAM check (company-cited):** Creative Cloud TAM $91B by 2027 (13% CAGR) vs ~$18.6B C&MP run-rate. Confidence low. **NEUTRAL.**

**Against the long:** challengers grow 2-4x Adobe; Figma is accelerating; Adobe's incremental share (64%) is well below its installed share (78%).

**UNAVAILABLE:** Google Trends (blocked; manual kit in data/); Adobe FY25 Creative/Document Cloud split and full-year C&MP/BP&C totals.
