# Google Form: Creative software and AI tool usage survey (ADBE channel check)

Workstream: ws6_channel_checks. Status: KIT ONLY, form not yet built or distributed as of 2026-10-02.
Target: 30+ responses. Estimated completion time: 3 minutes. All items after the screener are optional except where marked.

Form title: "How creative teams use design software and AI tools (3-minute student survey)"

Form description (shown at top): "This survey is for a student investment pitch competition. It asks about how your team uses design and creative software and AI tools. No confidential information is requested: no contract pricing, no client names, no internal financials. Responses are anonymous and reported only in aggregate."

Thesis-argument key: **B1** = price increases masking seat losses; **B2** = AI-driven seat compression; **U** = usage-based product adoption (Firefly, Firefly Services, GenStudio, Express, Acrobat AI Assistant); **C** = competition / work shifting to Canva, Figma, AI tools.

## Scoring notes for whoever tabulates the form

- Tabulate Q1 against Q3: if seats fall faster than headcount, that supports B1/B2; if seats track headcount, it weakens the bear case.
- Q6 "renewed as-is" plus Q7 scores of 1 to 2 weaken B1. Q6 "switched" or "cut seats" plus Q7 of 4 to 5 support B1.
- Q8 "regularly/daily" and Q10 "separate budget line" support the usage-shift leg of the long thesis.
- Q11 above 25% combined with Q4 "moved to non-Adobe tool" is the strongest WEAKENS signal for the long.
- Report only aggregates with n per cell. A sample of 30+ is still anecdotal; do not quote percentages to more than whole numbers.
