# Channel-check interview guide: Adobe Creative Cloud seat dynamics and AI tool adoption

Workstream: ws6_channel_checks. Status: KIT ONLY, no interviews conducted as of 2026-10-02.
Format: semi-structured, 15 minutes, 6 core questions plus probes. Three variants below (in-house designer, agency creative/producer, marketing-ops/creative-ops lead). Each variant is about one page.

## What we are testing

Long thesis on ADBE: the market over-fears that generative AI kills Creative Cloud. Two bear arguments this guide probes:

- **B1 (price masking seat loss):** reported Creative Cloud growth is price-driven (e.g., June 2025 Creative Cloud Pro increase, $59.99 to $69.99/month in North America) while seat counts are flat or down.
- **B2 (seat compression):** AI makes designers more productive, so companies cut design headcount and therefore seats.

Plus two secondary questions: (U) are Adobe's usage-based products (Firefly generative credits, Firefly Services API, GenStudio for Performance Marketing, Adobe Express, Acrobat AI Assistant) being adopted and paid for separately from seats; and (C) is work shifting to Canva, Figma, Midjourney, CapCut, ChatGPT image generation, etc.

## 30-second intro script (read verbatim)

"Thanks for making time. I'm a student on a team preparing an investment pitch on Adobe for a university competition. This is a 15-minute conversation about how your team uses design and creative software and how that has changed with AI tools. I am not asking for anything confidential: no contract pricing, no client names, no internal financials. I will not use your name or your company's name; responses are coded as, for example, 'a designer at a mid-sized retailer.' Can I take notes? And is it OK if I quote you anonymously?"

If they decline anonymous quotes, record only coded answers and leave `verbatim_quote` blank.

## Coding rules for interviewers (apply to every variant)

| Signal | SUPPORTS the long | WEAKENS the long |
|---|---|---|
| Team size (B2) | Design headcount flat/up; AI used to do more work with same people; hiring planned | Headcount cut explicitly attributed to AI productivity; further cuts planned |
| Seat count (B1/B2) | Seats flat/up, or changes track headcount only; seat count went up with new hires | Seats cut while headcount flat (downgrade to Standard, seat sharing, migration to Express or free tools) |
| Price reaction (B1) | Grumbled but renewed; price not a top-3 concern; "nobody is going to re-train on something else" | Actively shopped alternatives, downgraded tier, or dropped seats at renewal because of price |
| Firefly / credits / Services / GenStudio (U) | Regular credit use, bought extra credits, Firefly Services or GenStudio piloted or budgeted separately | Never used Firefly; credits unused; AI image generation done in Midjourney/ChatGPT and pasted into Adobe |
| Work share in other tools (C) | Canva/Figma used for low-end or UI work that Adobe never owned; Adobe still the "finishing" tool | Meaningful share (>25%) of previously-Adobe work now done elsewhere and seats dropped as a result |
| Would cut seats if... (all) | Trigger is hypothetical/far off (e.g., "if AI could do print-ready production") | Trigger is already happening or planned at the next renewal |

Code each interview overall as SUPPORTS / WEAKENS / MIXED in `supports_or_weakens_thesis`, and assign `confidence` (high: specific numbers given; med: directional only; low: vague or second-hand).

Do not lead. Ask the open question first, then probe. Record numbers where offered (seats, headcount, percentages). If a respondent gives a range, record the midpoint and note the range in `verbatim_quote` or notes.

## Variant A: In-house designer (brand, product, or marketing design team)

**Q1. Tell me about your team. How many designers are on it now versus 12 to 24 months ago, and what do you expect over the next 12 months?** (B2)
- Probe: Was any change linked to AI productivity, budget, reorg, or workload? Who made that call?
- Probe: Are open roles being backfilled? Are contractors or freelancers being used more or less?
- Probe: What does AI let you skip or speed up today (resizing, background removal, first drafts, copy variants)?

**Q2. How many Creative Cloud seats does your team have today versus a year ago, and what drove the change?** (B1/B2)
- Probe: Was it headcount, downgrading from Pro to Standard, sharing logins, or moving some people to Express or free tools?
- Probe: Which plan are you on (Pro, Standard, Teams, Enterprise)? Do non-designers (marketers, PMs) have full seats or Express?

**Q3. The Creative Cloud Pro price went from about $60 to $70 a month in mid-2025. How did your team or your IT/procurement react, and what happened at renewal?** (B1)
- Probe: Did anyone seriously evaluate an alternative? Which one? What stopped (or caused) a switch?
- Probe: Is the price increase something you noticed personally, or only procurement?

**Q4. How often do you or your team use Firefly (generative fill, text to image, generative credits)? Have you run out of credits or bought more?** (U)
- Probe: Does anyone use Firefly Services or GenStudio for Performance Marketing? Is that paid for separately from seats, and by whom (marketing vs. IT)?
- Probe: Do you use Acrobat AI Assistant or Express? Is it on the same bill?

**Q5. Roughly what share of your weekly design output is now done in Canva, Figma, Midjourney, CapCut, ChatGPT image generation, or similar tools instead of Adobe?** (C)
- Probe: Which tasks moved (social templates, UI, video, concept images)? Did any task move back to Adobe for finishing?
- Probe: Is the work that moved something Adobe used to do, or new work that did not exist before?

**Q6. What would have to happen for your company to cut Adobe seats materially?** (all)
- Probe: A specific price? A specific AI capability in another tool? A leadership mandate?
- Probe: Is that a 2026 or 2027 thing, or further out?

**Q7 (optional). Anything Adobe has shipped in the last year that changed your workflow for the better or worse?** (U)

## Variant B: Agency creative / producer

**Q1. How has the size of your creative and production staff changed over the last 12 to 24 months, and what do you expect in the next 12 months?** (B2)
- Probe: Was any change due to client budgets, AI productivity, or offshoring? Are you billing fewer hours for the same deliverables?
- Probe: Do clients now ask for AI-produced assets explicitly, or for discounts because "AI makes it faster"?

**Q2. How many Creative Cloud seats does the agency hold now versus a year ago, and why did it change?** (B1/B2)
- Probe: Headcount vs. tier downgrade vs. shared seats vs. freelancers bringing their own licences?
- Probe: Do freelancers and contractors get agency seats, or use their own subscriptions? (This affects seat counts without affecting output.)

**Q3. The Creative Cloud Pro price rose roughly $10 per seat per month in mid-2025. What did that mean for an agency with your seat count, and what did you do at renewal?** (B1)
- Probe: Did you pass it through to clients, absorb it, or trim seats?
- Probe: Did anyone evaluate Canva Enterprise, Figma, or an AI-first stack as a replacement for some seats?

**Q4. How much Firefly credit do you consume in a typical month, and do you ever run out?** (U)
- Probe: Have you used Firefly Services (API) or GenStudio for a client at scale (e.g., generating hundreds of ad variants)? Was that a separate line in the SOW or budget?
- Probe: For client work, is Adobe-generated content preferred for IP/indemnification reasons over Midjourney or ChatGPT?

**Q5. What share of deliverables now start or finish outside Adobe (Canva, Figma, Midjourney, CapCut, ChatGPT image generation, Runway, etc.)?** (C)
- Probe: Which tool for which task? Is Adobe still the final production step?
- Probe: Have any clients mandated a non-Adobe tool (e.g., client uses Canva for templates)?

**Q6. What would make the agency cut Adobe seats materially?** (all)
- Probe: Client pressure, price, a specific competitor feature, or headcount?
- Probe: Timing: at next renewal, or further out?

**Q7 (optional). Do you track hours saved from AI tools, and has that translated into fewer people or more work per person?** (B2)

## Variant C: Marketing-ops / creative-ops lead (owns licences, budgets, or the content supply chain)

**Q1. How has the size of the creative and content production team you support changed over the last 12 to 24 months, and what is planned for the next 12?** (B2)
- Probe: Is content volume up, flat, or down? Is more content being made per person?
- Probe: Has AI productivity been cited in any headcount or budget decision?

**Q2. How many Creative Cloud seats does the company hold now versus a year ago, and what drove the change?** (B1/B2)
- Probe: Headcount vs. tier (Pro vs. Standard) vs. seat-sharing vs. Express for non-designers vs. true-up at renewal?
- Probe: Which contract type (Teams or Enterprise/ETLA)? Who owns the renewal: marketing, IT, or procurement?

**Q3. When the Creative Cloud Pro price increased in mid-2025, what happened in your renewal or budget conversation?** (B1)
- Probe: Did the increase lead to a seat audit? Did you cut unused seats, downgrade, or hold?
- Probe: Did you get a quote from Canva, Figma, or another vendor? What was the outcome?

**Q4. Is any Adobe AI product budgeted separately from seats: Firefly credit packs, Firefly Services, GenStudio for Performance Marketing, Acrobat AI Assistant, Express?** (U)
- Probe: Who pays for it (marketing, IT, digital)? Is it growing year over year?
- Probe: Have you measured content-production cost per asset before and after Firefly or GenStudio?

**Q5. What share of the company's creative output is now produced in Canva, Figma, Midjourney, CapCut, ChatGPT image generation, or other non-Adobe tools?** (C)
- Probe: Is that replacing Adobe work or adding new volume (e.g., every rep making their own social posts)?
- Probe: Has Canva or Figma reached procurement as a direct Adobe replacement, or only as a complement?

**Q6. What would cause you to cut Adobe seats by 20% or more?** (all)
- Probe: Price, consolidation onto fewer vendors, AI tools inside Microsoft/Google suites, a headcount reduction?
- Probe: Is that scenario in your next 12-month plan?

**Q7 (optional). Where does Adobe sit in your next 12-month vendor roadmap: consolidate onto it, hold, or reduce?** (all)

"That's everything. Thank you. Is there anyone else on your team, or a peer at another company, who would be willing to do the same 15-minute call? And would you be comfortable if we used one or two anonymous quotes in the pitch?"
