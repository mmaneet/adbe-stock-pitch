# channel_check: findings

**Status: kit only, no data collected as of 2026-10-02.** This folder holds two tighter instruments (Survey A for in-house creative / marketing-ops leads and agency producers; Survey B for freelancers and students), Google Form text, outreach messages, CSV templates with EXAMPLE_ rows, and tally_chart.py, which tallies Yes/No and draws the Lennox-style Table 1 bar chart.

**Contentions mapped:** C1 (B-Q4), C2 (A-Q2 plus % change), C3 (A-Q3, A-Q5, B-Q2), C4 and its falsifier (A-Q4, B-Q3, B-Q5); concessions seat compression (A-Q1 vs A-Q6) and individual-subscriber weakness (B-Q1).

**Sample targets:** 10-15 Survey A interviews, 20+ Survey B responses.

**Caveat:** results will be small, non-random, and anecdotal; report as "x/n" colour, never extrapolate to Adobe's base. Confidence: n/a until data exists.
