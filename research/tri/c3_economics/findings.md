# C3 findings: Adobe can afford AI at scale; AI-native peers cannot (yet)

**Company (SUPPORTS, with a caveat).** Subscription gross margin 91.1% (FY25Q1) to 91.3% (FY26Q1), then 90.9% (Q2) and 90.4% (Q3 FY26, -0.8pt YoY). Cost of subscription grew 24% YoY in Q3; hosting/data-center "including AI inferencing" contributed 22pts (14 in Q1, 16 in Q2: accelerating). Incremental subscription GM in Q3 was 84%. Non-GAAP operating margin 44.0% (Q3, -2.3pt YoY) but the FY26 guide is held at ~45%; 9M FCF $7.47B (capex 0.9% of revenue). AI-first ending ARR lower bounds: >$125M (FY25Q1), >$250M (Q3), "more than tripled" (FY26Q1, no $), >$500M (Q2), >$650M (Q3, +150%). CFO (Q3 FY25): watches "cost per inference ... maniacally." Falsifier leg 1 is partially triggered: margins slip ~1pt as usage scales, but from 91%, with Semrush mix entangled.

**Peer (SUPPORTS).** Figma GAAP gross margin 91.5% (Q1'25) to 79.4% (Q1'26) and 83.7% (Q2'26); cost of revenue +117% YoY on "+$27.1M hosting costs relating to AI"; FCF margin 27% to 14%; beta AI products carry unmonetized inference. Canva (press-reported letter): Q2 revenue $921.9M (+25.2%), 2026 growth cut 30% to 20%, some features cost ~6x what users pay, cost per task cut ~90%, marks to $34.9-39.3B from $42B. Shutterstock data revenue -16%; Getty-Shutterstock merger terminated 2026-07-07.

**Industry/alt (NEUTRAL).** Adobe partner-model images via credits ~$0.08-0.10 vs Midjourney $0.03-0.05, Figma ~$0.04, API $0.03-0.07: 2-3x, but Adobe-native images are unlimited on paid plans (marginal $0), so leg 2 is not cleanly triggered. Flagship API prices have held at ~$0.03-0.04 since 2023.

**Primary (SUPPORTS).** Indemnity: Adobe (enterprise entitlement), Google (Imagen/Gemini/Veo), OpenAI (Enterprise/API, capped at 12-month fees), Canva (Enterprise only); Midjourney none. Deloitte 2024: IP concern cited by 35%.

**Falsifier: PARTIALLY TRIGGERED, not fatal.** -1pt subscription GM = -$0.54 FY26 EPS (2.2%); observed -0.8pt = -$0.44, offset by +$0.38 from +$400M AI-first ARR. Confidence medium-high (company), medium (peers).
