## Steps
1. **BLS verification (Task 1).** Unzipped `oesm25nat.zip`/`oesm24nat.zip` (national_M2025_dl.xlsx / national_M2024_dl.xlsx) and read TOT_EMP for 27-1024, 27-1011, 27-1014, 27-4032, 27-4021, 11-2021, 13-1161. All seven values match `research/ws2_seat_compression/data/bls_oews_employment_wide.csv` exactly (graphic designers 214,260 -> 197,830 = -7.67%; creative5 366,000 -> 348,240 = -4.85%). OOH page parsed for the 2025-35 outlook (-2%, -4,200 jobs, 253,100 jobs in 2025 on the projections base) and the AI wording. Results in `data/bls_verification.csv`. The HTML occupation pages themselves could not be fetched by any tool (bot redirect), so "verified" means verified against the BLS source data file, not the HTML page. Indeed Hiring Lab sector file has no "Design" category (47 sectors; closest are Arts & Entertainment, Marketing, Media & Communications), so the WS2 creative average is reused. `charts/creative_employment_vs_postings.png` combines WS2's OEWS index and Indeed index on one axis (both pre-COVID = 100; OEWS May-2019, Indeed Feb-2020) — it adds a single exhibit; the underlying series are WS2's.
2. **Freelance markets (Task 2).** Fiverr 6-K Ex.99.1 (Q4-25, Q1-26, Q2-26) and Upwork 8-K Ex.99.1 (Q1-26, Q2-26) read from sec.gov; sentences with AI + demand language extracted; quotes kept <25 words. Fiverr Business Trends Index: fiverr.com blocked; WebSearch snippets recorded with verified=N. Output `data/freelance_ai_statements.csv`.
3. **Adobe Stock (Task 3).** Transcripts grepped for "Stock" (excluding stock-based/repurchase). Q1 FY26 is the only call with Stock commentary; Q2/Q3 FY26 have none. Implied Stock YoY change derived from "instead of the 10.9% growth, it would have been approximately 11.2% growth": with T0 = $23.50B, T1 = $26.06B, S1 = $0.45B, solve (T1-S1)/(T0-S0) = 1.112 -> S0 = $0.468B -> Stock ARR change = -$18M (~-4%); rounding of the two growth figures gives a -2% to -8% range. Shutterstock (CIK 1549346) and Getty (CIK 1898496) FY2025 10-Ks and Q2-2026 10-Qs parsed for content/creative revenue, subscribers, downloads, data-licensing revenue and merger status; Getty 8-K/A (Item 1.02) confirms termination on July 7, 2026. Output `data/stock_licensing_trends.csv`.
4. **Individual subscribers (Task 4).** Quotes located programmatically in the transcript text with page numbers (from pypdf page breaks) and the nearest preceding speaker header; Q1 FY26 financial-section quote attributed to Daniel J. Durn (header at line 446), Q2/Q3 FY26 financial sections to Steve Day (interim CFO). WS1 decomposition (`research/ws1_price_volume/data/decomposition.csv`, C&MP base_20pct rows) cited: implied volume/mix growth 9.29% (FY25Q1) -> 6.09% (FY26Q3). Output `data/individual_subscriber_quotes.csv`.
5. **Net new ARR (Task 5).** Adobe's Q3 FY26 investor datasheet publishes Total Adobe ARR at three currency vintages. Like-for-like FY25 quarters at Dec-2025 rates: 23.50 / 24.08 / 24.74 / 25.66 (10-Q Q2 FY26 confirms 24.08). FY24 Q4 at Dec-2025 rates is not published; estimated as 22.61 x 1.0187 (average of the four FY25 restatement ratios) = 23.03, so Q1 FY25 net new at Dec-2025 rates (0.47) is an ESTIMATE; the as-reported (Dec-2024 rates) figure is 0.45. Q2 FY26 net new includes ~$480M Semrush (release, 10-Q); Q3 FY26 Semrush contribution not quantified by Adobe. Q4 FY26 implied = 25.66 x 1.102 - 27.50 = 0.78. Digital Media ARR basis also shown (FY24 Q4 revalued $17.22B per Q4 FY24 release). Output `data/net_new_arr_fy25_vs_fy26.csv`, chart `charts/net_new_arr_fy25_vs_fy26.png`.
6. **EPS (Task 6).** Conversion: $100M ARR x 45% incremental operating margin x (1 - 18% non-GAAP tax) / 390M diluted shares = $0.0946. Q3 FY26 diluted shares were 395M (datasheet); 390M assumes continued buybacks. A 75%-decremental column is shown because lost subscription revenue sheds little cost. Bases: teams/enterprise CC ARR $8.5B and individual CC ARR $7.0B are ESTIMATES (Adobe does not disclose; `research/tri/c1_mix/data/exposure_tiers.csv` did not exist when this was written — integrator should swap in Subagent A's tiers). AI-first offset: ending ARR ">$650M, +150% YoY" (Q3 FY26) implies >=$390M of YoY adds; ">$500M" at Q2 FY26 implies +$150M in Q3 alone. Output `data/concessions_eps.csv`, chart `charts/concessions_vs_ai_offsets.png`.

## Assumptions and limitations
- T/E and individual CC ARR split is not disclosed; all seat/individual EPS figures scale linearly with the basis (3% of $8.5B = $255M; if T/E is $9.5B, $285M).
- "Lower bound" AI-first adds use Adobe's "more than" disclosures; AI-first ARR partly overlaps CC Pro (Firefly credits bundled), so offset ratios are company-claim based.
- The ~$480M organic FY26 cut is the analyst's (Zukin) arithmetic that management did not dispute; management said "maybe half" is the deferred price action, i.e., deferred revenue, not churn.
- Shutterstock Q2-26 content decline (-17%) is partly a tough Envato-inclusive comp and "weakness in new customer acquisition"; Getty's subscriber decline (-25% LTM) coincides with a deliberate shift to committed solutions. Both are nonetheless consistent with transactional stock licensing shrinking.
- Fiverr/Upwork statements are company claims about their own marketplaces; the Bloomberry posting analysis is a secondary source (verified=N).
- Chart palette: the ENV-mandated palette (#1F3A5F, #2A9D8F, #E9A23B, #C0392B, #7F8C8D) fails the dataviz validator on lightness/chroma for navy/grey and warns on amber contrast (2.11:1). Kept for cross-workstream consistency; relief applied as required — every amber bar carries a direct value label, bars have surface gaps, and legends are present on all multi-series charts.
- No git operations run. Raw PDFs/HTML are in the scratchpad (`.../scratchpad/subE/`), not the repo.
