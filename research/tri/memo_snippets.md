## AI resilience paragraph (~185 words)

**Adobe is AI-resilient, and in places AI-synergistic.** The AI-exposed tier is a minority of revenue. Rebuilding the $27.5B ARR book from customer-group revenue, the FY25 Digital Experience disclosure and the $2.2B of enterprise Creative Cloud ARR disclosed in March 2025, individual Creative Cloud is $5.0–7.5B, 18–27% of ARR;1,8 documents are 28% and the marketing platform 25%, and documents grew faster than creative in FY26 (+16% vs +13%).1 Professional demand remains Adobe-denominated: 61% of 2025 graphic-designer postings require Photoshop and 62% Illustrator, against 11% Figma and 7% Canva.21 AI is multiplying the content Adobe's enterprise stack produces: Meta's advertisers using generative creative tools rose from over 1 million to over 8 million in seven quarters,15 Google generated about 70 million ad assets in one quarter,16 and WPP reports 2.5x asset output on flat headcount.17 Adobe absorbs this inside a 90% subscription gross margin that gave up 0.8 points in Q3 FY26,2 while Figma's gross margin fell from 91.5% to 79–84% and Canva cut its 2026 growth guide from 30% to 20% on AI serving costs.12,13 AI-first ARR above $650M, up more than 150%, adds about $0.38 of EPS a year;1,34 a 10% decline in the individual tier would cost $0.47–0.71.34

## Concessions paragraph (4 lines)

We concede three things. US graphic-designer employment fell 7.7% in the year to May 2025 and BLS now projects a decline that it attributes to AI design tools;19 3–5% seat loss in teams and enterprise is $0.24–0.40 of EPS.34 Adobe Stock (~$450M) is eroding, as Shutterstock's content revenue falls 17%;6,25 a 20% decline is $0.09. Management itself lowered second-half individual ARR by deferring price actions and routing users to freemium,5 and FY26 organic net new ARR is tracking $2.14B against $2.63B in FY25;3 the bear case for all three is $0.82 of FY27 EPS (3%), and AI-first additions cover only half of it,34 while search interest in cancelling Adobe ran at four times its 2019 level in 2026 (Appendix Fig. 1).26
